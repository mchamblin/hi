Good topics for a job-search portfolio:

1. **A hard decision.** What were the options, what did you choose, and how did it turn out?
2. **A lesson from a failure.** Hiring managers trust people who can describe their own mistakes clearly.
3. **How you work.** How you run a project, review code, or onboard someone new.

Keep it short. Five hundred words that someone finishes beats two thousand that nobody does.

Set `draft: true` in the front matter to keep a post out of the published site while you work on it.
